🐾 Privacy Policy

Naz & Pash's Cozy Home ("the game") is a cozy cat-care game. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It works fully offline.

Your game progress

Your progress (the kitties' stats, love hearts, stickers, settings and any birthday dates or name you type in) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device. If you use the optional backup code in Settings, the code is shown to you and only copied when you choose to copy it.

Deleting the app, or using Settings → Start over, erases this saved progress.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. On some phones it can use vibration for small taps, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, no personal data from children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
